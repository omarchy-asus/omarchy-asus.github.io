Sane laptop defaults for ASUS on Omarchy.

Install it and your ROG behaves like a laptop. No G-Helper, no Armoury Crate, no flipping modes by hand.

status: experimental · for: ROG Zephyrus G16 / G14 · tested: GA605KM

What you get

  • Great battery life. About 9 W watching YouTube on battery.
  • The RTX sleeps when it should. It wakes for a monitor or a game, not for nothing.
  • Hibernate that works. Leave it asleep unplugged and it hibernates instead of dying.
  • Keyboard and Slash LED control. Brightness and patterns, built in.
  • Quiet on battery. Fans off at idle.
  • Chargers just work. USB-C or barrel, it picks the right power mode.
  • Monitors just work. Native resolution, and windows stay put after a replug.
  • Stays asleep in your bag. The touchpad can't wake it.
  • See your Win-lock. Fn+Super lock state shows up instead of a dead key.
  • Speakers at full volume.

All of this runs on our G16 today. Some of it is still being packaged; see in progress.

The fixes target the Zephyrus G16 and G14 family. Most of the stack (asus-wmi, hid-asus, the NVIDIA driver, hibernate, Hyprland) is shared across ASUS models, so other ROG and TUF laptops are welcome too; see contributing.

What was broken before

Fn+Super, for example, is locked by the keyboard's firmware; stock Linux just eats the report. Most of these are like that: small, specific, and invisible until you trace them.

  • Close the lid, unplug, walk away. It's still there tomorrow: after 20 minutes asleep it hibernates, and opens back up where you left off. was: the battery drained flat in sleep, and hibernate never actually powered off (NVIDIA #1412, HibernateMode=shutdown)
  • It stays asleep in your bag. Brushing the touchpad won't wake it; a click will. was: 129k touchpad wake events in one boot (ASUF1209 wake disarmed)
  • The RTX stays out of the way. It wakes when you plug in a monitor or start a game, and sleeps the rest of the time. was: also woken for 20–25 s every time the battery dropped 1 % (NVIDIA #1299)
  • Video on battery is cheap. YouTube and mpv play on the Radeon's video engine, so neither the RTX nor the CPU has to work for it. was: software decode, or a woken RTX
  • It's quiet on battery. The fans stay off while you read, write or browse. was: 2,000–3,800 rpm at idle. Temps under sustained load in this mode aren't logged yet
  • Plug in a charger and it just does the right thing, whether it's USB-C or the barrel. was: asusd and power-profiles fighting over the profile and EPP
  • Plug in a monitor and it comes up at its native resolution, and your windows are where you left them after a replug. was: windows rendered off-screen after an HDMI replug (Hyprland #16140)
  • You know when the Super key is locked. Fn+Super locks it in the keyboard's firmware; we just pass that through, so it no longer looks like a dead key, and an on-screen toast says when it locks or unlocks. was: stock Linux's hid-asus driver eats the lock report, so Super silently stops working
  • The speakers go all the way up. was: ALC285 Master hid 12.75 dB of volume. PipeWire also runs at realtime priority now (rtkit)

How it works

  1. Write the fix the way upstream would, gated on the hardware it's for.
  2. Measure it on the laptop. If it doesn't measure up, it doesn't ship.
  3. Ship a signed build, and send the patch upstream with a link back.
  4. When upstream releases it, delete ours. If a rebuild fails, withdraw it and stock flows through.

Contributing

Got a G16, a G14, or another ASUS laptop? A lot is shared between models, so your fix may already be here, or yours may help ours. Open an issue with the output of cat /sys/class/dmi/id/product_name, uname -r and omarchy version, and what's broken. Small PRs tested on real hardware are welcome.

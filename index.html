What you get

  • Hibernate that works

    Close the lid, unplug, walk away. After 20 minutes asleep it hibernates, and opens back up where you left off.

    was: the battery drained flat in sleep, and hibernate never actually powered off (NVIDIA #1412, HibernateMode=shutdown)

  • The RTX sleeps when it should

    It wakes when you plug in a monitor or start a game, and sleeps the rest of the time.

    was: woken for 20–25 s every time the battery dropped 1 % (NVIDIA #1299)

  • Video decodes on the iGPU

    YouTube and mpv decode on the Radeon iGPU's video engine, the most efficient place for it, so the RTX stays asleep and the CPU stays idle. About 9 W for YouTube on battery.

    was: 14 W, with software decode or a woken RTX

  • Quiet on battery

    The fans stay off while you read, write or browse.

    was: 2,000–3,800 rpm at idle. Temps under sustained load in this mode aren't logged yet

  • Stays asleep in your bag

    Brushing the touchpad won't wake it; a click will.

    was: 129k touchpad wake events in one boot (ASUF1209 wake disarmed)

  • Chargers that work

    Game on USB-C or a smaller barrel charger without stutter. It holds charging while the charger can't keep up, so power stays steady.

    was: on USB-C, the adapter flapped on and off about once a second, charging toggled with it, and the EC clamped the CPU to 735 MHz

  • Monitors just work

    Native resolution, and your windows are where you left them after a replug.

    was: windows rendered off-screen after an HDMI replug (Hyprland #16140)

  • You can see your Win-lock

    Fn+Super locks Super in the keyboard's firmware. We pass the state through and show a toast, so it no longer looks like a dead key.

    was: stock hid-asus ate the lock report, so Super silently stopped working

  • Keyboard and Slash LEDs

    Brightness and patterns from the G16 Control widget in the bar.

  • Speakers at full volume

    The ALC285 gets its whole range, and PipeWire runs at realtime priority.

    was: Master hid 12.75 dB of volume

The panel: omarchy-asus plugin

One panel in the Omarchy bar for the laptop's hardware: keyboard and lid Slash LEDs; per-charger keyboard, refresh rate, screen backlight and CPU power limits; battery charge limit; Fn+Super. Each section hides when your laptop doesn't have it. It's an Omarchy plugin, so it installs and updates the Omarchy way:

omarchy-plugin-add https://github.com/omarchy-asus/omarchy-asus-plugin --enable

Source and docs: omarchy-asus/omarchy-asus-plugin. Written on the ROG Zephyrus G16; needs asusd for the LEDs, charge limit and power limits.

How it works

  1. Write the fix the way upstream would, gated on the hardware it's for.
  2. Measure it on the laptop. If it doesn't measure up, it doesn't ship.
  3. Ship a signed build, and send the patch upstream with a link back.
  4. When upstream releases it, delete ours. If a rebuild fails, withdraw it and stock flows through.

Contributing

Got a G16, a G14, or another ASUS laptop? A lot is shared between models, so your fix may already be here, or yours may help ours. Open an issue with the output of cat /sys/class/dmi/id/product_name, uname -r and omarchy version, and what's broken. Small PRs tested on real hardware are welcome.
